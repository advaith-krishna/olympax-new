Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our programs, admissions, timings and fees.

Olympax Sports Academy is located near the Convent in Nellimoodu, Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala. We're easily accessible and provide a safe, professional training environment for all age groups.

We offer a diverse range of programs including sports (badminton, cricket, archery), martial arts (boxing, kickboxing, Muay Thai, karate, Wushu, Kung Fu), fitness and wellness (yoga, Zumba, weight loss, general fitness), and a kids summer camp.

We offer programs for children, students and adults. Some programs are open to all ages, while selected programs begin from age 6, 8, 10, 12 or 14 depending on the activity. Each program is tailored for the right stage of development.

The summer camp is a 2-month program for children aged 6 to 14, scheduled Monday to Friday from 9:30 AM to 4:00 PM. It includes a mix of sports, martial arts, fitness activities and confidence-building exercises.

You can reach us by calling +91 94477 93301 or sending a WhatsApp message. Our team is available to answer all your questions about programs, batch timings, fees and enrollment procedures.
